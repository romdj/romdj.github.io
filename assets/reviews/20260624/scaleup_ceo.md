### 1. Snap verdict
Reads as a senior architect with a leadership thesis pinned on top. Fit score **2/5**.

### 2. Would I take the call?
**No.** Every quotable proof-point is an architecture deliverable — "Authored the IAM Architecture Vision", "Architecture lead for a 12-person Nike team", "Backend platform contributor" — and none of them say *I ran an engineering org, shipped a product line, and owned the P&L conversation*. That is the seat I am hiring for.

### 3. What works
- The wedge is real and sharply written: *"Agentic AI practitioner, with a focus on operating-model design for an AI-native organization."* Most candidates I see are theorizing about this. I would want to talk to someone who actually has 15 CLAUDE.md configs in production-adjacent use.
- The agentic-AI bullet is the only one that smells like an operator, not a reviewer: *"Most recent artifact: `/resume-review`, a multi-agent system with isolated reviewer subagents and a sycophancy audit."* That is a builder's sentence. More of this, please.
- Regulated-environment fluency is concrete and credible: *"anchored in NIS2, DORA, and eIDAS 2.0"* and the Philips bullet *"one of the first medically-compliant cloud platforms (FHIR, HIPAA, FMEA-governed), 1B+ device connections per year."* If I sell into hospitals, banks, or utilities, this is leverage.
- Nike bullet finally has a delivery anchor: *"Daily delivery with on-call accountability."* Five words that distinguish him from the pure-PowerPoint architect crowd. He needed those words.
- Real linguistic range — *"Languages: French Native, English Native, Dutch Professional, Spanish Professional"* — useful in BeNeLux scale-up commercial conversations.

### 4. What does not work
- The headline title is **"Engineering Leader"** but every Selected Impact bullet leads with an architecture verb: *"Authored the group-level IAM Architecture Vision"*, *"Set group-wide architecture governance"*, *"Authored the long-term vision for [...] Customer Centricity Platform"*, *"Architecture lead for a 12-person Nike team"*. Four of six bullets start with *Authored / Set / Architecture lead*. This is an architect's resume with a leader's hat photoshopped on.
- The Profile is buzzword-dense and content-thin: *"M-shaped engineering leader at group level: deep architecture, deep engineering, broad business."* "M-shaped" is jargon I have to decode. *"Solver mindset, Scaler ambition: 0→1 by instinct, 1→N by design; built on enterprise rigor"* is a slogan, not evidence. I want to see *"I ran X people, shipped Y, here is the revenue/cost outcome"* — there is no such line on this page.
- Zero P&L, zero hire/fire, zero team-size-grew-from-N-to-M. The biggest "people" number on the page is *"12-person Nike team"* — and even there he is *"Architecture lead"*, not the engineering manager. For a Head-of-Eng seat, the absence of a "I owned the team, the cadence, and the performance bar" sentence is disqualifying on its own.
- The agentic-AI bullet undersells in the only place that matters: *"an operating-model framework for AI-native engineering currently in development."* "Currently in development" is a tell. For a CEO, that reads as a side project. Either it is operational somewhere with a result, or do not promise the framework on the resume.
- The footer renders *"Last updated: 06/24/26-09-2026"* — a broken date on the document selling me a leader who claims to ship. A small thing, but I notice. If you cannot ship your own resume cleanly, I am going to wonder about the cadence you set for the team.

### 5. Toughest interview questions I would ask
1. You have never held an Engineering Manager title. Walk me through the last time you ran a performance conversation that ended in a separation, including who initiated it and what you said in the room.
2. Your Nike bullet says *"Architecture lead for a 12-person team"*. Who owned the people-management — hiring, promotions, raises, terminations — for those 12? If not you, who, and what was your actual decision authority?
3. You frame *"review over creation"* as the new bottleneck. In your last quarter at Elia, give me one concrete artifact where you implemented verification capacity as a system — gate, contract, evaluator — and tell me the measurable shift in throughput or defect rate.
4. You list *"15+ CLAUDE.md configurations"* and *"/resume-review, a multi-agent system"*. Why are your shipped agentic artifacts personal-scale rather than from inside a 300-team TSO? What blocked you from deploying this at Elia, and what would unblock you at my company in week one?
5. You prefer the *"freelance management-company"* employment structure. I am hiring a Head of Engineering to own a 24-month roadmap with my board. Convince me you are not optimizing for tax efficiency at the expense of skin in the game.

Kill the "M-shaped" Profile and replace it with one outcome sentence and one operating-model sentence.

Run agentic workflows daily; building the operating model for small teams with outsized scope, held together by interfaces instead of process."*

### 7. Final recommendation
**Pass.** Strong reviewer, weak operator signal. I would forward to my Chief Architect search, not my CTO search. If the next iteration leads with shipped product-and-people outcomes instead of authored documents, I will reread it.
