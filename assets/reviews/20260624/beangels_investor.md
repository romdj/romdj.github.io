### 1. Snap verdict
Polished group-architecture résumé from a national TSO with a credible AI-native thesis but thin operator evidence on the visible page. Bet score: **2/5**.

### 2. Operator signal
**Absent on the visible page.** The candidate is positioned entirely as a salaried/contracted architect. The visible bullets are all *inside an employer's perimeter*: "Authored the **group-level IAM Architecture Vision** at Belgium's national TSO", "Set **group-wide architecture governance** at Belgium's national TSO", "**Architecture lead for a 12-person Nike team**". The closest thing to a build is "Most recent artifact: `/resume-review`, a multi-agent system" — that is a personal tool, not a venture. The "Ongoing Projects" section in the data file (Temps d'Arrêt Studio, 3point-game-nhl-standing, Eindhoven Technology Meetup) is set `display: false` and therefore *not on the page I scan*. No services company, no property, no product venture is anywhere visible. For an angel lens, the page reads as a senior employee with side scripts, not a builder.

### 3. Would I invest in a venture they were starting?
**No.** Nothing visible tells me this person has ever closed a customer, written an invoice, made payroll, or carried personal capital risk — and that is the bet I'd be making.

### 4. What works
- **Regulated-enterprise scope is real and quantified.** "Unified 3 fragmented identity stacks and 5 authorization mechanisms for **300+ external market participants** across Belgium and Germany, anchored in NIS2, DORA, and eIDAS 2.0." This is the kind of credential that opens doors at any regulated BeNeLux scale-up selling into utilities, banks, or health systems. Sellable.
- **One concrete, dated AI-native artifact, not a buzzword.** "Most recent artifact: `/resume-review`, a multi-agent system with isolated reviewer subagents and a sycophancy audit." The "sycophancy audit" phrase signals someone who has actually used these systems and noticed the failure modes — that's rarer than the LinkedIn-AI cohort.
- **The three-sector spread is honest and warm.** "Medtech … Philips, Extra Horizon, FibriCheck", "Energy & public sector … Elia (current)", "Sports tech … Nike". Three lanes, three real employers in each, gives a portfolio company multiple angles of warm intro.
- **The M-shape framing is a usable thesis.** "**M-shaped engineering leader at group level: deep architecture, deep engineering, broad business.**" If they can defend the second M live, it differentiates from the standard T-shaped architect pitch.

### 5. What does not work
- **The title is generic and undersells.** "**Engineering Leader**" as the headline — for an investor who reads 50 of these a year, that's a Tuesday. There's no role specificity (Head of? CTO? Co-founder?) and no domain. The tagline "Architecture · Direction-setting · Operating Model for an AI-native organization" is three nouns, not a wedge.
- **The Profile is three abstractions stacked.** "**M-shaped engineering leader at group level**", "**Agentic AI practitioner**", "**Solver mindset, Scaler ambition: 0→1 by instinct, 1→N by design; built on enterprise rigor.**" — I cannot find a single verb of *commerce* here. No P&L, no customer, no €, no team-grown-from-X-to-Y, no shipped product. It is direction-setting prose about direction-setting.
- **The Selected Impact bullets are all "Authored", "Set", "Architecture lead", "contributor".** "**Authored** the … Architecture Vision", "**Set** group-wide architecture governance", "**Architecture lead** for a 12-person Nike team", "Backend platform **contributor**". For a portfolio CTO seat I need verbs like *shipped, sold, hired, grew, cut, turned around*. Architects author; operators ship.
- **The AI-native bullet over-indexes on artifacts that are own-bench.** "**15+ CLAUDE.md configurations** across personal projects" and a `/resume-review` tool. That tells me this person is *thinking* about AI-native orgs, not yet that they have *run* one. McKinsey/Deloitte/Anthropic-style operating-model literacy is in the back-pocket context doc — none of it has hit a customer.
- **No founder, no €, no exit, no failure visible.** The deliberate decision to hide Zero to One Solutions SRL, property, and Maison Renaud may be tactical, but on the page-I-actually-scan there is zero operator evidence. As written, this résumé does not separate Romain from any other senior Belgian solution architect.

### 6. What I would want to see in a 30-minute conversation
1. **The financial story behind Zero to One Solutions SRL.** Annual revenue range, number of clients, biggest engagement, what got priced wrong the first time. If the answer is "it's just my freelance vehicle", that's a tell.
2. **One end-to-end commercial story.** A specific client, what they paid, what changed for them, how it ended. Not "presented to C-Level".
3. **A live walk-through of the AI-native operating model on a portfolio-company shape.** Given a 30-person Series A medtech, what does the org chart look like in 18 months, where is the verification capacity, what breaks first.
4. **Evidence of a hard conversation.** A time they fired/exited/PIP'd/refused a hire — anything that demonstrates the people-management muscle the target_profile itself flags as untested.
5. **Their honest read of the freelance-vs-permanent choice for a CTO seat.** If they pick freelance for tax reasons, that signals optionality over commitment, and I need to hear how they'd answer that from a board.

### 7. One thing I would change in the resume
**Surface one operator credential on the one-pager — Zero to One Solutions SRL — even if the numbers stay private.** It's the single change that flips operator signal from Absent to Partial.

Before (Profile, line 1):
> "M-shaped engineering leader at group level: deep architecture, deep engineering, broad business."

After:
> "M-shaped engineering leader at group level: deep architecture, deep engineering, broad business. Operates a Belgian services company (Zero to One Solutions SRL) alongside group-level architecture work — builder by default, not only by title."

That one line is the difference between "another architect" and "someone with skin in the game".

### 8. Final recommendation
**Worth a coffee.** I would take the call because of the regulated-enterprise scope and the AI-native thesis — both are scarce in the BeNeLux pool. I would not push to a portfolio-CTO meeting on this page alone, and I would not put my own money behind a Romain-led venture until I'd heard the commercial story the résumé is deliberately hiding.
