GreenFlux, part of DKV Mobility, plays a central role in Europe’s rapidly growing e-mobility ecosystem, connecting over a million charging points across the continent. The Director of Engineering scales the platform and drives the integration of multiple Charge Point Management Systems into a unified backend landscape, helping shape a scalable, future-proof architecture across the group.

About GreenFlux

GreenFlux, headquartered in Amsterdam since 2011, has been a key player in smart charging solutions since joining DKV Mobility five years ago. Together, they combine advanced cloud-based charging technology with a strong international mobility network.

The platform connects hundreds of thousands of EV drivers to more than a million charging points across Europe. As the organization evolves within a broader ecosystem, multiple technologies and platforms are being aligned into one integrated backend. DKV Mobility brings over ninety years of experience in high-volume, cross-border operations; GreenFlux brings the charging software expertise. The combination puts the company at the heart of both innovation and large-scale transformation.

The environment combines the pace and ownership of a scale-up with the reach and ambition of a leading international organization, and the opportunity to contribute directly to the energy transition. A team of 120 people from 36 nationalities drives this forward, in a culture where pragmatism and idealism reinforce each other, and personal growth is a core part of the work.

Vacancy: Director of Engineering

The Director of Engineering leads the engineering organization, responsible for consistent, high-quality delivery in a complex and evolving context. Strategy must be effectively translated into execution – and that is precisely what this role delivers.

The core mission is to further improve engineering velocity and quality in a highly dynamic market. By optimizing processes, removing bottlenecks, and strengthening ways of working, the Director of Engineering enables teams to deliver faster while safeguarding platform stability – on an Azure-based infrastructure – refining what has become the “GreenFlux

At the same time, the role plays a key role in the broader architectural evolution within DKV Mobility. Engineering teams are working across platform boundaries, requiring clear prioritization and strong technical judgment to balance integration efforts with ongoing platform development. Bringing these priorities – platform excellence and system integration – together, the Director of Engineering ensures that short-term delivery and long-term scalability complement each other.

“We need someone who can bridge the gap between high-level strategy and hands-on execution: a pragmatic, outcome-driven leader who focuses on results over process, enables teams to move fast, and takes ownership at every level. Someone who thinks in product as much as in engineering, with a clear sense of how the business creates value for customers.” – Thomas Rahmen, Managing Director Tech & Product

Reporting to Thomas Rahmer, Managing Director Tech & Product, the Director of Engineering leads a team of more than sixty engineers and works closely with product and business stakeholders in an international environment. The role offers substantial scope to shape both the engineering organization and the technical backbone of one of Europe’s most consequential e-mobility platforms.
