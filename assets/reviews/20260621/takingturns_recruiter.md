# Taking Turns Recruiter Review — 2026-06-21

> **Scope:** exec-summary one-pager (`/`) only. Detail page (`/detail/`) was out of scope for this pass.
> **Resume state at review time:** title was *"Cross-functional Leader: Engineering, Architecture, Direction"*, tagline *"I make small, AI-augmented teams safe to run fast"*, 5 Selected Impact bullets pre-rewrite.

### 1. Snap verdict
Reads as a senior architect with leadership ambition, not yet a placeable Head of Eng/CTO. **Placeability score: 2/5.**

### 2. Strongest placement lane
Lead Architect / Principal Architect or Head of Architecture at a Series B-C Belgian energy-or-regulated-medtech scale-up selling into enterprise/TSO/utilities buyers (think: grid-edge software, identity/compliance plays under NIS2/DORA, regulated digital-health BaaS in BeNeLux). Not a Head of Engineering seat at a 30-300-person scale-up — that requires team-ownership signal this page does not carry.

### 3. 30-second rejection lines
A hiring committee will flag these in their first pass:

1. **No team ownership signal anywhere.** Title reads *"Cross-functional Leader: Engineering, Architecture, Direction"* — three lanes is none. The Selected Impact bullets are all architectural artefacts (*"Authored the DIF / IAM Architecture Vision"*, *"Established group-wide API and messaging standards"*, *"delivering 3 intermediate solution designs"*). No "led a team of X", "hired Y engineers", "ran the roadmap for Z product line". A CTO/Head of Eng committee will reject in 30 seconds.
2. ***"Architecture lead for a 12-person team at Nike"*** — "architecture lead for" is not "led". This phrasing tells a recruiter the candidate was the architect inside a 12-person team, not the manager of one. Two different jobs, two different price points.
3. ***"Influence without formal authority"*** is repeated as a strength: *"Unblocked 2 development teams from a quarter-long delay … Initiative taken without formal authority"* and *"Cross-team initiative leadership and influence without formal authority"*. To a Belgian leadership-search committee, this reads as "has never had the seat" — the exact gap the candidate is trying to bridge, served back to them in their own words.
4. **The tagline lands as marketing, not as a leader's POV.** *"I make small, AI-augmented teams safe to run fast."* Belgian boards in energy, medtech, and regulated finance are conservative on AI-native operating-model claims. Without a concrete result behind it ("shipped X in Y weeks with a 4-person team using…"), it reads as buzz.
5. **Enterprise/TSO-heavy gravity.** *"Belgium's national TSO"*, *"300+ external market participants"*, *"NIS2 / DORA / eIDAS 2.0"*, *"IT4IT library"*, *"Architecture Review Board"*, *"TOGAF / ATAM"* — this is an enterprise-architect résumé. A scale-up CEO will read "slow, governance-first, will not ship". The candidate's own target-profile gap (#2 — enterprise background, scale-up chaos concern) is not bridged on this page.
6. **Birthdate (25/06/1991) and driver's license on a leadership one-pager.** Reads as junior-format. Top-of-funnel signal that the candidate has not yet calibrated to the seat they want.

### 4. What works
1. *"Authored the DIF / IAM Architecture Vision at Belgium's national TSO, unifying 3 fragmented identity stacks and 5 authorization mechanisms across 300+ external market participants in Belgium and Germany, anchored in NIS2 / DORA / eIDAS 2.0."* — concrete scope, named regulation, C-level visibility. The strongest single bullet on the page and the spine of the architect-lane pitch.
2. *"1B+ device connections per year"* and *"multi-million daily events at peak"* — scale numbers a Belgian recruiter can quote to a client without verification anxiety. Sticks.
3. Sector triangulation is clean: *"Medtech … Philips, Extra Horizon, FibriCheck"*, *"Energy & public sector … Elia (current), ABVV-FGTB, Engie"*, *"Sports tech … Nike"*. Matches the three target lanes precisely. Network-warmable in all three.
4. *"Most engineering leaders are either deep technologists who have never run an AI-native org, or AI-hype operators with no governance spine. I bring both."* — sharp wedge, well-written. Will land with the right CEO. Lands flat with the wrong one.
5. Brussels (*"Uccle (Brussels), Belgium"*) plus French/English native and Dutch B1.2 — the BeNeLux mandate-fit is unambiguous.

### 5. What does not work
1. *"Cross-functional Leader: Engineering, Architecture, Direction"* — three nouns in a title is the universal tell of someone unsure which seat they're applying for. Committees read this as positional confusion.
2. The Selected Impact section contains **zero people-leadership outcomes.** Five bullets, all architecture artefacts and documents. *"Established group-wide API and messaging standards affecting ~100 engineering teams"* — affected, not led. *"Adopted formally through the IT4IT library"* — process-vocabulary, scale-up-hostile.
3. *"Hands-on agentic AI as a daily collaborator"* — under "AI & data", alongside *"historical ML / CV with TensorFlow; reinforcement-learning work (AWS DeepRacer)"*. The AI-native operating-model thesis the tagline promises is nowhere in the Selected Impact. Tagline writes a cheque the body of the résumé does not cash.
4. *"I work close to the business and lead through initiative and competence rather than positional authority."* — the candidate's own honest gap (no formal mgmt title) rebranded as a virtue. Belgian search committees see straight through it. They pay for the seat, not for the workaround to not having had it.
5. The Capabilities block is an IC architect's skills list. A Head-of-Eng one-pager would lead with operating model, hiring/scaling, P&L/budget ownership, vendor and roadmap accountability — none present.
6. Certifications block on a leadership one-pager (Neo4j, AWS Developer Associate, Agile SCRUM Foundation, Udemy FHIR) is IC-grade and pulls the seat downward.

### 6. Positioning fixes to land more calls

**Fix 1 — Title.**
- Before: *"Cross-functional Leader: Engineering, Architecture, Direction"*
- After: *"Head of Engineering — Regulated Scale-ups (Energy, Medtech)"*. Pick one seat. If the candidate is not ready to claim Head of Engineering, write *"Lead/Principal Architect — Regulated Platforms"* and pursue the architect lane honestly at the corresponding price.

**Fix 2 — At least one Selected Impact bullet must be people/delivery, not architecture artefact.**
- Before: *"Unblocked 2 development teams from a quarter-long delay by delivering 3 intermediate solution designs in parallel with the strategic POC track. Initiative taken without formal authority…"*
- After: *"Owned the delivery track for 2 dev teams (~12 engineers) stuck on a quarter-long block; shipped 3 designs in parallel to the strategic POC and brought both teams back to cadence in 6 weeks."* Drop *"without formal authority"* — it is the candidate's tell.

**Fix 3 — Carry the AI-native thesis into the body, or drop the tagline.** Right now the tagline lands as a promise the body doesn't keep.

**Fix 4 — Strip junior-format fields.** Remove birthdate, driver's license, and the entire Certifications block from the one-pager.

**Fix 5 — Profile paragraph 1.** Replace the passive architect-coded opener with an active leadership-coded one.

### 7. Market price estimate (Belgium)

- **As pitched today (architect lane, the seat the page actually sells):** EUR 600-750/day freelance via management company, or 95-120k base + 10-15% variable permanent. Freelance comfortably feasible.
- **If positioning is fixed → Head of Engineering / VP Eng at Series B-C in BeNeLux:** EUR 700-900/day freelance, or 130-170k base + 15-25% variable + light equity permanent. Freelance increasingly contested at this level.
- **CTO at a 30-100 person Series A-B (the candidate's stated ambition):** EUR 800-1100/day or 150-200k + equity. **Not currently bridgeable from this one-pager.**

Net: the freelance lean is feasible in the architect lane and the lower Head-of-Eng band. It fights gravity at CTO.

### 8. One thing I would change in the résumé

Replace the title block to lock the seat and the price.

- **Before:** *"Romain Lussier — Cross-functional Leader: Engineering, Architecture, Direction — I make small, AI-augmented teams safe to run fast."*
- **After:** *"Romain Lussier — Head of Engineering, Regulated Scale-ups (Energy & Medtech, BeNeLux) — Enterprise-grade governance, scale-up delivery speed."*

### 9. Final recommendation
**Hold.** Strong raw material (TSO scope, regulated-sector credibility, sector triangulation, BeNeLux geo-fit) but the page sells architect, not leader. Re-pitch when the fixes land; at that point I would move to **Match to niche mandate** (Head of Architecture / Lead Architect in regulated BeNeLux scale-ups) and watch for the right CTO brief to test the upgraded positioning.
