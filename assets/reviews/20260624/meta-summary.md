## Top 3 action items, ranked by impact

### 1. Replace the Profile with one outcome sentence and one operating-model sentence.

All three reviewers flagged the current three-beat Profile. The Recruiter and CEO each volunteered concrete rewrites that share the same DNA: lead with a quantified, checkable claim; then state the wedge in plain English; drop invented taxonomy.

**Before (current):**
> M-shaped engineering leader at group level: deep architecture, deep engineering, broad business.
>
> Agentic AI practitioner, with a focus on operating-model design for an AI-native organization.
>
> Solver mindset, Scaler ambition: 0→1 by instinct, 1→N by design; built on enterprise rigor.

**After (synthesis of CEO + Recruiter proposed rewrites):**
> Engineering leader operating at group-architecture altitude across ~100 teams at Belgium's national TSO. Currently authoring the IAM, API, and messaging spine for 300+ regulated market parties. Builds engineering organisations where small, AI-augmented teams ship fast without diverging — held together by hard interfaces, contracts, and real-time governance rather than process ceremonies.

Same wedge, scale-anchored, no invented vocabulary, lands the AI-native operating-model thesis in plain English.

### 2. Reframe the AI-native bullet from personal-bench to operating-model authorship.

All three reviewers landed on the same critique: "personal projects" + "in development" are tells.

**Before:**
> **Multi-year practitioner of agentic AI in engineering work**: **15+ CLAUDE.md configurations** across personal projects; an operating-model framework for AI-native engineering currently in development. Most recent artifact: `/resume-review`, a multi-agent system with isolated reviewer subagents and a sycophancy audit.

**After (Recruiter's proposed rewrite):**
> Designs operating models for AI-native engineering: verification-first workflows, agentic governance patterns, and interface-led divergence control. Practises daily on agentic toolchains and ships the patterns back as reusable scaffolding.

Risks losing the `/resume-review` concrete artifact reference. Compromise: keep one sentence on the framework, one sentence on the artifact, no "personal projects" framing.

### 3. Promote the people-leadership signal: add headcount / authority quantifiers wherever they exist honestly.

CEO and Recruiter both noted the Nike bullet under-claims. Recruiter's proposed rewrite combines the Nike scope with the Elia 20+ team architectural-authority figure, and explicitly owns the "seeking the seat that formalises this" framing rather than leaving it to be inferred.

**Before:**
> **Architecture lead for a 12-person Nike team** at the EMEA distribution hub (Laakdal, multi-million daily events at peak). Deconstructed a 15-year-old monolith; migrated to Centiro and then Blue Yonder. Daily delivery with on-call accountability.

**After:**
> Led the architecture function for a 12-engineer Nike platform team (Laakdal EMEA hub) with daily delivery and on-call accountability; held architectural authority across 20+ adjacent product teams at Elia. Seeking the seat where that authority becomes formal line leadership.

The "seeking the seat" half-sentence puts the no-prior-Eng-Mgmt-title bridge on the page rather than letting committees discover the gap themselves.

## Cross-run trajectory

The three runs (2026-06-21, 2026-06-22, 2026-06-24) all sit in the same 2/5
to 2.5/5 band. Score deltas have not moved meaningfully despite three rounds
of in-session rewrites:

- 2026-06-22 (leadership-repositioning wave): CEO 2 · Investor 2 · Recruiter 2.5
- 2026-06-24 (Profile compression + Selected Impact tighten): CEO 2 · Investor 2 · Recruiter 2.5

The unchanged verdict over three iterations is itself signal: **wordsmithing
the Profile is not the binding constraint.** The same four critiques keep
returning regardless of how the page reads — they point to evidence the
resume genuinely does not have on it today (people-leadership numbers, an
org-deployed AI-native artifact, an explicit operator credential, and a
title-of-record that matches the headline).

The next meaningful score movement will come from changes the resume can
*evidence*, not from changes to how it *phrases*. Specifically: add one
ship-with-real-headcount AI-native datapoint, surface one operator credential
on the page, or accept the "architect" framing and pivot the target seat to
Head of Architecture (which the Recruiter explicitly endorsed today).
