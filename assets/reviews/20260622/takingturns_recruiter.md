# Taking Turns Recruiter Review — 2026-06-22

> **Scope:** exec-summary one-pager (`/`) only.
> **Resume state at review time:** title *"Engineering Leader: Architecture, Direction-setting, Operating Model for AI-native engineering"*, tagline dropped, 6 Selected Impact bullets including Z8 (multi-year agentic AI practitioner + `/resume-review` artifact).

### 1. Snap verdict
Reads as a senior group architect with an AI-native angle, not a leadership hire. Placeability **2.5/5**.

### 2. Strongest placement lane
Head of Architecture / Principal Architect at a Series B–C regulated-tech scale-up in BeNeLux selling into enterprise — specifically energy-tech, B2B identity/compliance (NIS2 / DORA / eIDAS), or medtech-platform. Not a Head-of-Engineering or CTO seat as-is. If a client specifically asks for "AI-native operating model architect" or "Chief Architect with AI mandate", this jumps a tier.

### 3. 30-second rejection lines

1. **"Architect, not a leader."** The title says it itself: *"Engineering Leader: Architecture, Direction-setting, Operating Model for AI-native engineering"* — three of four nouns are architect-language. Every Selected Impact bullet is an architecture artifact: *"Authored the DIF / IAM Architecture Vision"*, *"Authored the long-term organizational vision"*, *"Architecture lead for the most senior team at Nike"*. No P&L, no headcount owned, no hire/fire, no budget.

2. **No people-management evidence at all.** The closest the page gets is *"Cross-team initiative leadership across functions; coaching and technical mentoring"* — that is IC-with-influence language. A hiring committee scanning for "managed X engineers, grew team from Y to Z, ran performance cycles" will find nothing.

3. **Enterprise/regulated DNA all the way down.** *"300+ external market participants"*, *"NIS2 / DORA / eIDAS 2.0"*, *"~100 engineering teams, ~50 applications, ~70 data products"*, *"Architecture Review Board"*. A Series B founder reads this and thinks "will spend six months writing standards before shipping."

4. **The AI-native pitch leans on personal projects, not org outcomes.** *"15+ CLAUDE.md configurations spanning multiple domains"* and *"/resume-review, a multi-agent system…"* are hobbyist artifacts. No "ran an agentic pilot at Elia that shipped X" or "reduced team-to-output ratio by Y."

5. **Acronym density a non-specialist committee will bounce off.** *"DIF / IAM"*, *"ARB-ratified"*, *"ADEMAR and CCP"*, *"FMEA"*, *"ATAM / ADR"*, *"TSO"*. Reads as in-house enterprise jargon, not portable leadership language.

### 4. What works

1. **The "both-worlds" sentence is a genuine differentiator.** *"Most engineering leaders are either deep technologists who have never run an AI-native org, or AI-hype operators with no governance spine. I bring both."* The one line that earns a second read.

2. **Tier-1 brand stack lands fast.** *"Belgium's national TSO"*, *"Nike"*, *"Philips HSDP"* — three logos that a Belgian recruiter will recognize and a hiring committee will not question.

3. **The scale numbers are real and specific.** *"1B+ device connections per year"*, *"multi-million daily events at peak"*, *"300+ external market participants"*. Concrete, defensible at reference-check.

4. **Three named sectors with credible credentials in each.** *"Medtech — Philips, Extra Horizon, FibriCheck"*, *"Energy & public sector — Elia, ABVV-FGTB, Engie"*, *"Sports tech — Nike"*. Lets me match against three different mandate types.

5. **Brussels-based, multilingual.** *"Uccle (Brussels), Belgium"*, *"French — Native, English — Native, Dutch — Professional literacy (B1.2)"*. Belgian profile clients ask for.

### 5. What does not work

1. **No team-size or budget anywhere.** *"Architecture lead for the most senior team at Nike (12 people…)"* says lead **of** architecture for a 12-person team, not led 12 people. There is no number on this page saying "I managed N engineers" or "owned €Xm budget." For a leadership pitch, that is fatal.

2. **"Authored a vision" is repeated three times.** *"Authored the DIF / IAM Architecture Vision"*, *"Authored the long-term organizational vision for the Customer Centricity Platform"*. Authoring visions is not running an org. A CEO reading this thinks: PowerPoint, not P&L.

3. **The AI-native proof point is a side project named after this very exercise.** *"Most recent applied artifact: /resume-review, a multi-agent system with isolated reviewer subagents, anti-flattery enforcement, and sycophancy audit."* Putting your job-hunt tool on your one-pager as flagship AI evidence will get noticed — and not in the way intended.

4. **Date stamp is broken.** *"Last Updated: 06/22/26-13-2026"*. Sloppy footer on a leadership profile. Committees notice.

5. **Profile paragraph buries the lead in compliance language.** *"Strongest in regulated, high-stakes environments where business proximity, technical depth, and architectural governance must coexist."* Positions the candidate **away** from scale-up speed, not toward it.

### 6. Positioning fixes to land more calls

**Fix 1 — Strip "Architecture" out of the title.**
- Before: *"Engineering Leader: Architecture, Direction-setting, Operating Model for AI-native engineering"*
- After: *"Engineering Leader — AI-Native Operating Models | Scaled Governance | Regulated Domains"*

**Fix 2 — Replace "Authored a vision" with action-and-outcome verbs.**
- Before: *"Authored the DIF / IAM Architecture Vision unifying 3 fragmented identity stacks…"*
- After: *"Drove unification of 3 fragmented identity stacks and 5 authorization mechanisms across 300+ market participants. Owned alignment with Business, CIO, CISO. Multi-year roadmap now in delivery under my technical direction."*

**Fix 3 — Put a people/scope number on the page, even a soft one.**
- Before: *"Cross-team initiative leadership across functions; coaching and technical mentoring."*
- After: *"Set technical direction for ~100 engineers across 10 scrum teams; led delivery of cross-cutting standards without line authority; coached architects and tech leads across 20+ product lines."*

**Fix 4 — Move the "both-worlds" sentence to the very top of the Profile.** That paragraph is your wedge. Make it the headline under your name.

**Fix 5 — Cut the `/resume-review` bullet or replace it with an org-level AI outcome.** Only keep if you can frame it as a piloted artifact deployed in a team you did not personally own.

**Fix 6 — Fix the footer.** *"Last Updated: 06/22/26-13-2026"* → *"Last updated: June 2026"*.

### 7. Market price estimate (Belgium)

For the realistic lane (Head of Architecture / Principal Architect, regulated scale-up or scale-up-adjacent enterprise):
- **Permanent total comp:** €130k–€170k base + 10–20% bonus, possibly small equity. ~€145k–€200k on-target.
- **Freelance day rate:** €900–€1,150/day. ~€190k–€250k gross annual at standard utilization.

For the *stretch* lane (Head of Engineering at a Series B BeNeLux scale-up) — if the positioning fixes above land:
- **Permanent:** €150k–€200k base + bonus + meaningful equity.
- **Freelance day rate:** €1,000–€1,300/day, but most scale-up Head-of-Eng seats are perm-only with equity. **Freelance lean is not feasible in this bracket** unless the role is explicitly fractional or interim.

Net: the freelance preference is **feasible at the architect bracket, problematic at the leadership bracket**. The candidate cannot have "freelance lean" and "Head of Engineering at a scale-up" simultaneously in this market. Pick one.

### 8. One thing I would change in the resume

Rewrite the Profile opening sentence.

- Before: *"I lead engineering direction at group level, setting the technical principles and governance that steer organizations toward shared business outcomes, bridging enterprise-grade rigor with AI-native practice."*
- After: *"I run engineering organizations where small, AI-augmented teams move fast without diverging. I bring enterprise-grade governance and hands-on agentic practice — the rare combination scale-ups need when they sell into regulated buyers."*

First sentence sells **running an org**, not **setting principles**. That is the entire delta between architect and leader.

### 9. Final recommendation

**Match to niche mandate.** Hold the file for an "AI-native operating-model architect" or "Head of Architecture, regulated scale-up" brief. Not an active pitch for Head-of-Engineering or CTO seats until the positioning fixes above are applied and at least one concrete people-leadership or P&L data point is added to the page. The one thing that stops me pitching to my best three scale-up clients today: there is not a single number on this page that proves the candidate has ever *run* anything, only that they have *designed* things.
