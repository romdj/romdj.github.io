### 5. What does not work

- **"M-shaped engineering leader at group level: deep architecture, deep engineering, broad business"** — invented vocabulary in the first line of the profile. The Belgian market reads "T-shaped" reflexively; "M-shaped" reads as the candidate explaining his own framework instead of trusting the market's. First-line cost, every reader pays it.
- **"Solver mindset, Scaler ambition: 0→1 by instinct, 1→N by design; built on enterprise rigor."** Three abstractions stacked. Zero verifiable fact. A hiring committee reads "0→1 by instinct" and asks "what did you take from 0 to 1?" — the resume has no answer. Belgian scale-up founders distrust slogans without proof.
- **The AI-native pillar is asserted but not proven at org scale.** Bullet 6: *"Multi-year practitioner of agentic AI in engineering work: 15+ CLAUDE.md configurations across personal projects; an operating-model framework for AI-native engineering currently in development."* "Personal projects" and "in development" together kill the thesis. The candidate's own context doc argues the wedge is *"enterprise-grade governance + hands-on agentic practice"* — the resume signals the first half cleanly and the second half thinly. Committees skimming for AI-native operating-model leadership will land on a competing profile with the same governance spine and one shipped agentic-org case study.
- **"Architecture lead for a 12-person Nike team … on-call accountability"** — this is the closest thing to a leadership proof point on the page and it is buried as bullet 4 of Selected Impact. "Lead" without headcount, P&L, hiring authority, or team-survival metrics will not survive a Head-of-Eng probe.
- **Title-of-record everywhere on the detail page is Solution Architect.** *"Solution Architect (Enterprise Level)"*, *"Solution Architect"*, *"Technical Architect"*, *"Solution Architect / Software Engineer."* The one-pager "Engineering Leader" headline is contradicted by every dated role below it. The mismatch is what triggers the "architect cosplaying as a leader" reaction the candidate is trying to outrun.

### 6. Positioning fixes to land more calls

**Fix 1 — Drop the invented vocabulary, name the role you want.**
- *Before:* "M-shaped engineering leader at group level: deep architecture, deep engineering, broad business."
- *After:* "Engineering leader operating at group-architecture altitude across ~100 teams. Comfortable owning technology direction, regulated-environment governance, and the operating model end-to-end."

**Fix 2 — Replace the slogan with a proof-anchored thesis.**
- *Before:* "Solver mindset, Scaler ambition: 0→1 by instinct, 1→N by design; built on enterprise rigor."
- *After:* "Builds engineering organisations where small, AI-augmented teams ship fast without diverging — held together by hard interfaces, contracts, and real-time governance rather than process ceremonies."
That is the candidate's own wedge from the context doc. Put it on the page.

**Fix 3 — Reframe the AI bullet from hobby to operating-model authorship.**
- *Before:* "Multi-year practitioner of agentic AI in engineering work: 15+ CLAUDE.md configurations across personal projects; an operating-model framework for AI-native engineering currently in development. Most recent artifact: `/resume-review`, a multi-agent system with isolated reviewer subagents and a sycophancy audit."
- *After:* "Designs operating models for AI-native engineering: verification-first workflows, agentic governance patterns, and interface-led divergence control. Practises daily on agentic toolchains and ships the patterns back as reusable scaffolding."
Drops "personal projects" and "in development" — both are tells.

**Fix 4 — Promote the people-leadership signal and quantify it.**
- *Before:* "Architecture lead for a 12-person Nike team at the EMEA distribution hub …"
- *After:* "Led the architecture function for a 12-engineer Nike platform team (Laakdal EMEA hub) with daily delivery and on-call accountability; held architectural authority across 20+ adjacent product teams at Elia. Seeking the seat where that authority becomes formal line leadership."
The "seeking the seat" half-sentence is the bridge framing from the target profile — own it on the page rather than letting committees infer the gap.

**Fix 5 — Change the title under the name.**
- *Before:* "Engineering Leader"
- *After:* "Engineering Leader · Group Architecture & AI-native Operating Model" — or, more honestly: "Group Architect moving into Engineering Leadership."
The second is riskier and more credible. Either one beats a one-word claim contradicted by every dated role.
